The steps every game goes through are in [docs/RELEASE.md](../../docs/RELEASE.md). This page
has Tide Garden's own answers for them. Building the app is in [ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md).

npx tsx tools/store-assets.ts games/tidegarden store screens

## The editions

| | Ads only | Full |

## Play Console

- **App name:** `Tide Garden: Reef Match 3`.
- **Category:** Games, Puzzle.
- **Screenshots:** `1-splash.jpg` to `6-looks.jpg`, in that order.
- **In-app products** (full edition): the ids, names and descriptions are at the end of
  `listing.txt`. The browser version shows $0.99 to $9.99 as placeholder prices.
- **Countries:** all. Nothing for sale is random.

## Play it before every upload

On the phone, from the internal test link: the first level by swiping and by tapping two
pieces, a swap that makes no match, a wave, a bubble bomb and a pearl, two special pieces
swapped together, the claw, the tide and the hermit crab, a tool from a rewarded ad when the
bag is empty, running out of moves and taking five more with a rewarded ad, ending a dive and
trying again, reaching a level and doubling its sand dollars with an ad, an ad break (they
start after three levels reached, after the first five minutes of a session, and only when
the results close), a baby turtle crawling down to the sea, the last level of a zone and its
creature coming home, the daily dive, a water and a buddy bought with sand dollars, the back
button during a dive, and switching to Thai.

## Facts for the content rating

Answer the IARC questionnaire yourself, honestly; these are the facts to answer from:

- No violence, fear, sexual content, bad language, drugs, alcohol, tobacco or crude humour.
  Players swap pieces of a coral reef to line them up, and help baby turtles down to the sea.
- No gambling and no simulated gambling: there are no bets and no wagers, and the sand
  dollars the game pays can only be spent in the game. Nothing can be won or cashed out.
- Players cannot talk to each other or share content. The app does not share their location.
- Full edition: yes, in-app purchases of digital goods, with no random items.
- Ads-only edition: no purchases.
