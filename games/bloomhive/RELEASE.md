# Releasing Bloom Hive

The steps every game goes through are in [docs/RELEASE.md](../../docs/RELEASE.md). This page
has Bloom Hive's own answers for them. Building the app is in [ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md).

```sh
npx tsx tools/store-assets.ts games/bloomhive store screens
```

## The editions

| | Ads only | Full |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Players get | rewarded ads and ad breaks; honey, tools, every sky and every hat earned by playing | the same, plus honey packs, Keeper’s Kit, No Ad Breaks, Meadow Collection, cloud saves and Google sign-in |

## Play Console

- **App name:** `Bloom Hive: Bubble Shooter`.
- **Category:** Games, Puzzle.
- **Screenshots:** `1-splash.jpg` to `6-daily.jpg`, in that order.
- **In-app products** (full edition): the ids, names and descriptions are at the end of
  `listing.txt`. The browser version shows $0.99 to $9.99 as placeholder prices.
- **Countries:** all. Nothing for sale is random.

## Play it before every upload

On the phone, from the internal test link: the first level by dragging to aim and by tapping
where to shoot, a shot that bounces off a side, swapping the next pollen, a group that pops
and buds that fall with a baby bee in them, the nectar jar filling into a bomb, the rainbow,
the bomb and the sunbeam, a tool from a rewarded ad when the pouch is empty, running out of
pollen and taking five more with a rewarded ad, a bud coming down to Pip and five more pollen
blowing the lowest rows away, ending a round and trying again, reaching a level and doubling
its honey with an ad, an ad break (they start after three levels reached, after the first
five minutes of a session, and only when the results close), wax, honey, a dandelion puff and
a butterfly on their first levels, the last level of a zone and its flower blooming in Pip’s
garden, the daily meadow, a sky and a hat bought with honey, the back button during a round,
and switching to Thai.

## Facts for the content rating

Answer the IARC questionnaire yourself, honestly; these are the facts to answer from:

- No violence, fear, sexual content, bad language, drugs, alcohol, tobacco or crude humour.
  Players shoot pollen at flower buds to pop them, and free baby bees from the buds.
- No gambling and no simulated gambling: there are no bets and no wagers, and the honey the
  game pays can only be spent in the game. Nothing can be won or cashed out.
- Players cannot talk to each other or share content. The app does not share their location.
- Full edition: yes, in-app purchases of digital goods, with no random items.
- Ads-only edition: no purchases.
